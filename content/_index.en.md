## About

Yusheng Zhou is a cross-disciplinary environmental epidemiologist and data scientist. He studies the health effects of extreme heat, greenspace, and other environmental exposures, particularly on cardiovascular morbidity and mortality. He develops and applies statistical and data science methods for large-scale cohort and administrative data, including distributed lag non-linear models (DLNM), multilevel and geospatial modelling, and machine-learning pipelines. His work uses the UK Biobank, the Canadian Longitudinal Study on Aging, and national population registers. This research quantifies how climatic and environmental exposures shape cardiovascular risk across populations, with particular attention to sex, gender, and socioeconomic determinants. He is proficient in R and Python, with a record of national-level funding (CIHR) and first-author publications in environmental health, climate and health, and cardiovascular epidemiology.

## Research interests

- Extreme heat, greenspace, and other environmental exposures
- Cardiovascular morbidity and mortality
- Distributed lag non-linear models (DLNM), multilevel and geospatial modelling, and machine-learning pipelines
- Sex, gender, and socioeconomic determinants
- Register-based epidemiology
